# Squeek Arena

A Pokemon-style quiz battle game built on Squeekchain, the hamster-powered toychain on Ethereum Sepolia.

Pick a trainer, connect your wallet (one gacha pull per wallet address, recorded locally in the browser), and open your gacha box (one box in a thousand hides AURUM, the rare hamster that attacks directly with no quiz), and climb a five-boss ladder against Squeektoshi, who takes the names of knowledge gods as he grows smarter: BRAGI Lv 1, THOTH Lv 2, SARASWATI Lv 3, ATHENA Lv 4, NOUS Lv 5. Questions come from real Squeekchain facts plus live questions generated from the public API, and every boss gets an attack buff while Squeektoshi is really running on his wheel. Extra gacha pulls are pet NFTs minted on Sepolia (see the companion Squeek Arena Pets contract): the first pull is a free mint that costs only gas, extra pulls cost 0.002 ETH each. Beat NOUS and you are certified Squeekchain fams.

Static site, no backend, no wallet. Progress is stored locally in the browser and is never claimed to be onchain.
